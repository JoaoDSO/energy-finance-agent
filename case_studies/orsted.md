# Ørsted: funding an investment-heavy period

**Question:** How much of Ørsted's construction investment was covered by operating cash, and what do its disclosures leave unresolved about funding?

**Sources:** [FY2025 annual report](https://cdn.orsted.com/-/media/annual-2025/annual-report-2025.pdf?hash=D5AF74AEDCCB4EB539AAD875E368374A&rev=34ff0f324dc1429bac1bc84382c30a9d) and [Q3 2026 interim report](https://cdn.orsted.com/-/media/q32026/orsted-interim-financial-report-q3-2026.pdf?hash=BF8F727EC2E6ECAB7946480D89175E26&rev=2d35d305456e45b1b9a421c86bf40a95). PDF page references below are one-based physical pages.

## Results

| 9M2026 measure | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Operating cash flow | DKK 7.97bn |
| Gross investment cash outflow | DKK 28.53bn |
| Operating cash / gross investment | **27.93%** |
| Free cash flow including divestments | DKK −12.54bn |
| September net debt | DKK 37.74bn |

The investment-coverage calculation divides operating cash flow by gross investment outflow ([Q3 report, p. 9](https://cdn.orsted.com/-/media/q32026/orsted-interim-financial-report-q3-2026.pdf?hash=BF8F727EC2E6ECAB7946480D89175E26&rev=2d35d305456e45b1b9a421c86bf40a95#page=9)). The free-cash-flow bridge combines operating cash, investment and divestment proceeds on that page. The net-debt bridge also reconciled to the reported DKK 37.74bn ([pp. 9–10](https://cdn.orsted.com/-/media/q32026/orsted-interim-financial-report-q3-2026.pdf?hash=BF8F727EC2E6ECAB7946480D89175E26&rev=2d35d305456e45b1b9a421c86bf40a95#page=10)).

**Conclusion:** Operating cash did not cover gross investment in this period. Equity issuance, farm-downs and disposals provided substantial support, but those are different from recurring operating cash. Historical figures do not establish whether future funding is sufficient.
