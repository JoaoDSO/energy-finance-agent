# Vestas: stronger earnings, weaker cash conversion

**Question:** Did stronger earnings and a larger order backlog translate into cash through September 2026?

**Sources:** [FY2025 annual report](https://www.vestas.com/content/dam/vestas-com/global/en/investor/reports-and-presentations/financial/2025/fy-2025/Vestas%20Annual%20Report%202025.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf) and [Q3 2026 interim report](https://www.vestas.com/content/dam/vestas-com/global/en/investor/reports-and-presentations/financial/2026/q3-2026/261111_59_Company_Announcement.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf). PDF page references below are one-based physical pages.

## Results

| Nine months to September | 2026 | 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| EBIT before special items | €1,056m | €487m |
| Operating cash flow | €573m | €988m |
| Adjusted free cash flow | −€340m | −€42m |

Operating cash flow fell **42%**, while adjusted free cash flow declined **€298m**. The 9M2026 operating cash-flow statement shows €1,435m before working-capital changes and an €862m working-capital outflow ([Q3 report, p. 18](https://www.vestas.com/content/dam/vestas-com/global/en/investor/reports-and-presentations/financial/2026/q3-2026/261111_59_Company_Announcement.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf#page=18)). The adjusted free-cash-flow bridge reconciles to −€340m ([p. 7](https://www.vestas.com/content/dam/vestas-com/global/en/investor/reports-and-presentations/financial/2026/q3-2026/261111_59_Company_Announcement.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf#page=7)).

**Conclusion:** Earnings improved, but cash conversion weakened in the comparable nine-month periods. Vestas attributed much of the working-capital increase to Offshore activity in progress. Backlog represents expected future revenue; it does not establish when cash will arrive.
